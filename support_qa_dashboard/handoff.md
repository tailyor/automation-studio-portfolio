# Support QA Exception Dashboard Handoff

This is a sample/demo dashboard for support, customer success, and AI support-agent leads. Use it as a proof artifact when pitching ticket QA, escalation reporting, refund-risk review, or weekly support operations summaries.

## What It Shows

- Ticket volume by support channel, issue type, and team
- Weighted QA score
- Human review workload
- Refund or billing risk
- First-response and resolution timing
- Escalation and needs-review queue
- QA score trend and exception reasons

## Suggested First Paid Milestone

Price range:

- 150-300 USD if the client provides one clean anonymized ticket export.
- 300-600 USD if there are multiple support tools, complex QA rubrics, or custom escalation rules.

Scope:

- One support export
- KPI cards
- Two charts
- Exception queue
- Basic filters
- One revision
- Handoff notes with metric definitions

Do not promise direct Zendesk, Intercom, Stripe, or internal tool access in the first milestone. Ask for a redacted export or mock file first.

## Safety Notes

- Do not ask for live support-tool credentials in the first conversation.
- Ask for anonymized sample data first.
- Keep customer names, ticket text, account IDs, payment details, and private policy notes out of samples.
- If the workflow requires AI evaluation, production integrations, or billing/refund actions, quote discovery first.
